Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 10, 2026

We built NoExcuse to help you take control of your money — not to profit from your data. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can delete it all.

1. Who We Are

NoExcuse is a personal budgeting application built and operated by Oluwemimo Favour, based in Nigeria. You can contact us at: wemimofavour@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

When you create an account and use NoExcuse, we collect the following:

  • Your name and email address — used to create and identify your account
  • Your budget categories and spending limits — set by you, stored to power your dashboard
  • Your expense entries — amounts, reasons, and dates you log manually
  • Your streak and badge data — to track your progress
  • Your accountability partner preferences — email of partners you invite
  • Monthly archive history — past months saved when a new month begins

We do not collect your bank account details, card numbers, BVN, or any payment information.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the NoExcuse service to you:

  • To display your budget dashboard and categories
  • To calculate your spending and send you budget alerts
  • To maintain your streak and award badges
  • To notify your accountability partners when you choose to share updates
  • To generate your monthly report card

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Where Your Data is Stored

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), with servers located in Europe West 2 (London). Firebase is compliant with GDPR and major international data protection standards.

Your data is protected by:

  • Firebase Authentication — passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Firestore security rules — you can only access your own data
  • HTTPS encryption — all data in transit is encrypted

5. Accountability Partners

If you add an accountability partner, they will be able to see your total budget usage percentage and receive notifications based on your chosen alert level. They cannot see your individual expense details or reasons. You can remove a partner at any time from the Partner page.

6. Your Rights Under the NDPA 2023

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your name or email at any time
  • Deletion — permanently delete your account and all associated data
  • Objection — opt out of any automated processing that affects you
  • Portability — receive your data in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at wemimofavour@gmail.com or use the Delete Account option in your dashboard settings.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data including expenses, categories, archives, streak data, and partner connections will be permanently deleted within 24 hours.

8. Children's Privacy

NoExcuse is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
