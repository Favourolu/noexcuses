Features

Everything you need. No excuses.

🔐
Evidence Gate Unique

Before any expense is logged, you must provide a reason or justification. This single feature eliminates unconscious impulse spending — something no other budget app does. You become fully aware of every financial decision you make.

Personalized Shame Messages

We don't just notify you — we remind you of your own financial goals in the moment. It's designed to sting just enough to make you reconsider.
